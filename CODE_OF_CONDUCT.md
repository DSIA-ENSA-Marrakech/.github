# Code of conduct

We aim to maintain a respectful, inclusive, constructive learning and engineering environment.

- Treat others with respect, welcome different experience levels, and critique work rather than people.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, disclose private information or misrepresent others’ contributions.
- Credit sources and collaborators; disclose conflicts of interest and correct errors.
- Raise concerns privately with a club officer or relevant school representative. Avoid publishing personal details in an issue.

Club leadership should review concerns fairly, protect confidentiality as far as possible and determine appropriate action according to the club and school procedures.
